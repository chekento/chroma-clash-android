## Short description

## Full description
How precisely can you perceive and reproduce color? Chroma Clash turns color perception into a long-term skill game.

Match a target color with your own mix and improve your visual accuracy round by round. Scoring is based on CIE Lab and CIEDE2000 ΔE, making subtle color differences far more meaningful than a simple RGB distance.

YOUR PROGRESSION
• 100 ranks across 10 leagues
• 40 unlockable achievements
• rising XP requirements for long-term progression
• career statistics for accuracy, score, streaks and completed rounds

GAME MODES
• Standard Run with 10 rounds
• Marathon with 25 rounds
• Endless for longer sessions
• Adaptive Difficulty that responds to rank and performance

PRECISE COLOR CONTROL
The HSV color picker covers the full color gamut. Hue, saturation and value can be adjusted deliberately so every challenge remains technically solvable.

OFFLINE-FIRST
Chroma Clash runs locally on your device. No user account is required to play. Progress and statistics are stored locally.

Chroma Clash is designed for players who want to train color perception, visual precision and personal bests through repeatable challenges.
