# Google Play Preview Asset Requirements

Prepared for the current Chroma Clash release.

## Required core assets

### App icon
- PNG, 32-bit with alpha
- 512 × 512 px
- max 1024 KB
- do not add ranking, price or misleading badges/text

### Feature graphic
- JPEG or 24-bit PNG, no alpha
- 1024 × 500 px
- keep important content away from crop-sensitive outer edges

### Phone screenshots
- at least 2 screenshots required to publish the store listing
- JPEG or 24-bit PNG, no alpha
- min dimension 320 px, max dimension 3840 px
- max dimension may not be more than twice the min dimension
- for stronger game merchandising, use at least 3 actual gameplay screenshots at 1080×1920 portrait or 1920×1080 landscape

## Prepared asset set

A companion local asset package was generated with:
- `icon-512.png`
- `feature-graphic-1024x500.png`
- `01-hub.png`
- `02-gameplay.png`
- `03-color-picker.png`
- `04-achievements.png`
- `05-rank-ladder.png`

The screenshots use the actual Chroma Clash interface. Career progress is seeded only to demonstrate the rank and achievement systems in the store imagery; the UI and features shown are part of the current app.
