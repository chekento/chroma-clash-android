# Privacy Policy — Chroma Clash

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Chroma Clash is designed as an offline-first Android game.

## Data collection and sharing

The current release does not collect, transmit or share personal data with the developer or third parties.

- No account is required.
- No analytics SDK is included.
- No advertising SDK is included.
- No tracking pixel is included.
- No remote AI or API service is required for gameplay.
- No Android runtime permissions are requested.

## Local game data

Career progress, ranks, achievements, scores, streaks and settings are stored locally on the user's device in Android WebView storage. Android application backup is disabled in the release manifest.

Removing the app or clearing its application data may permanently remove this local progress.

## Children and accounts

The app does not provide user accounts, social login or developer-operated profiles. It does not knowingly collect personal data from children or adults because the current release does not collect personal data at all.

## Changes

If future versions add online services, analytics, advertising, accounts, cloud saves or other data-processing features, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration must be updated before release.

## Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent using the developer contact information published with Chroma Clash on Google Play.
